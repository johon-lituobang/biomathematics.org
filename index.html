关于我们 About Us

简介 Brief

生物数学研究所致力于以统计学搭建生命与数学之间的桥梁，探索生命现象的规律。我们的研究涵盖从分子生物学到生态系统的多个尺度，致力于发展新的理论方法、工具和实验数据解析框架，推动数学与生命科学的深度交叉。研究所所有研究成果均通过区块链网络永久存档，确保学术记录的不可篡改与长期可访问。

The Institute of Biomathematics aims to bridge life and mathematics with statistics to exploring the principles of living systems through mathematics and computation as its core tools. Our research spans multiple scales, from molecular biology to ecosystems, and is committed to developing novel theoretical methods, computational tools, and experimental data analysis frameworks that advance the deep intersection of mathematics and the life sciences. All research outputs of the Institute are permanently archived on the blockchain, ensuring academic records remain tamper‑proof and permanently accessible.
